# Wolffsohn Interactive Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to Wolffsohn Interactive projects and the communities built around them, including BorderlessMC and future projects published under the Wolffsohn Interactive name.

It is adapted from Contributor Covenant 3.0.

## Our Pledge

Wolffsohn Interactive is committed to maintaining welcoming, safe, and constructive communities for people participating in good faith.

Everyone should be treated with respect regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, language, beliefs, or other personal characteristics.

## Expected Behavior

Community members are expected to:

- Treat other people with respect and communicate in good faith.
- Respect different viewpoints, experiences, and levels of technical knowledge.
- Give and receive constructive feedback respectfully.
- Keep technical disagreements focused on the work rather than the person.
- Take responsibility for their own words, actions, and contributions.
- Respect clearly stated personal boundaries.
- Credit the work and sources used in contributions.
- Keep discussions reasonably relevant to the project or community space.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment or continued unwanted attention after being asked to stop.
- Personal attacks, insults, threats, intimidation, or deliberately hostile behavior.
- Discrimination or demeaning stereotypes.
- Inappropriate sexualized behavior or content.
- Publishing or using another person's private information without permission.
- Impersonating another person or intentionally misrepresenting another person's statements or work.
- Evading moderation actions through alternate identities or accounts.
- Encouraging violence or other harm.
- Spam, deceptive promotion, or deliberately disruptive behavior.
- Other conduct that reasonably threatens the safety or well-being of the community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to community spaces operated by Wolffsohn Interactive or its individual projects, including:

- GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Project-managed chat or community platforms.
- Project documentation and collaborative spaces.
- Other spaces where someone is officially representing Wolffsohn Interactive or one of its projects.

Individual projects may publish additional project-specific rules. Those rules supplement this Code of Conduct and do not replace it.

## Reporting

Possible Code of Conduct violations should be reported privately to the maintainers of the relevant Wolffsohn Interactive project using the contact information published by that project or its maintainers.

For BorderlessMC, reports may be directed to the project maintainer, **ValiantGiant985**, using the contact information published on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Do not publish sensitive reports, private information, or evidence containing private information in a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as practical. Information about a report will be kept private to the extent reasonably possible while investigating and addressing the issue.

## Enforcement

Wolffsohn Interactive project maintainers may take action based on the severity, context, repetition, and impact of a violation.

Possible actions include:

1. **Warning** — A private warning explaining the issue and the behavior expected going forward.
2. **Temporary restriction** — Temporary limits on participation in particular project spaces or interactions.
3. **Temporary suspension** — Temporary removal from project or Wolffsohn Interactive community spaces, with conditions for returning when appropriate.
4. **Permanent ban** — Permanent removal from project or Wolffsohn Interactive community spaces when serious or repeated violations make continued participation unsafe or unreasonable.

This enforcement ladder is a guideline. Maintainers may skip steps when the severity of an incident requires it.

Maintainers may remove, edit, lock, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, commits, discussions, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.
